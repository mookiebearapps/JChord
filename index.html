JoyChord

Player 1 plays chords, Player 2 plays melody. Use controllers, touch, mouse or keyboard.

JoyChord

Controller: D-pad or left stick = chords. With one controller the right stick = melody; with two, the second controller plays melody. LB/RB or stick clicks change instrument (single controller: LT/RT for melody).
Keyboard: W A S D or arrows = chords (Q/E instrument). I O L . , M J U = melody around K (Y/P instrument).
